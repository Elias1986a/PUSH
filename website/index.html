Private by design

Your voice and your keystrokes never leave your Mac.

There's no cloud to trust with your words, because they never reach one.  The speech model runs on your Mac's Neural Engine and the typing model on its GPU.  Your audio is thrown away the moment it becomes text, and what you type is read only to make a suggestion.

No cloud

Speech recognition and suggestions run entirely on your Mac.  Nothing you say or type is uploaded, or used to train anyone's model.

Questions

Does it need the internet?

Only to download the models, once: about 600 MB for Talk, and 1.3 to 5.3 GB for TYPE depending on the model you pick.  After that, both work with Wi-Fi off.

Which Macs?

Any Apple silicon Mac (M1 or later) on macOS 15 or newer.  The largest typing model wants 16 GB of memory; the others run on any of them.

What does “every update” mean?

Every version of PUSHTYPE for the Mac we release, free, for as long as we make it.  No paid upgrades, no subscription.

Is it really private?

Your audio, your transcripts, and what you type never leave your Mac.  The privacy policy lists the few things that do connect: model downloads, update checks, license checks with Polar, and iCloud sync of your settings and dictionary (on unless you switch it off).

Does TYPE learn from what I write?

Only if you switch on “Learn from my writing and voice”.  Then it keeps what you type and dictate, encrypted on your Mac, so suggestions sound like you.  The writing is never sent to us, and Delete All erases it.  If you choose, it's shared with your other Macs through your own iCloud, encrypted with a key only your Apple Account has.  Your name and About you note follow you there too.

Can I use just one of them?

Yes.  Talk and TYPE each have their own switch in the menu bar and in Settings.  Use one, the other, or both.

What if it's not for me?
