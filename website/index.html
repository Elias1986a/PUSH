Questions

Does it need the internet?

Only to download the models, once: about 600 MB for dictation, and 1.3 to 5.3 GB for Type depending on the model you pick. After that, both work with Wi-Fi off.

Which Macs?

Any Apple silicon Mac (M1 or later) on macOS 15 or newer. The largest typing model wants 16 GB of memory; the others run on any of them.

What does “every update” mean?

Every version of PUSHTYPE for the Mac we release, free, for as long as we make it. No paid upgrades, no subscription.

Is it really private?

Your audio, your transcripts and what you type never leave your Mac. The privacy policy lists the few things that do connect: model downloads, update checks, licence checks with Polar and optional iCloud sync.

Does Type learn from what I write?

Only if you switch on “Learn from my writing and voice”. Then it keeps what you type and dictate, encrypted on your Mac, so suggestions sound like you. It's never synced or sent, and Delete All erases it.

Can I use just one of them?

Yes. Dictation and Type each have their own switch in the menu bar and in Settings. Use one, the other, or both.
