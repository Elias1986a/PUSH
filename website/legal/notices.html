Speech models (downloaded on first use, not bundled)

PUSHTYPE doesn't ship model weights.  When you press Download in Settings, PUSHTYPE fetches the model you picked straight from its public Hugging Face repository onto your Mac.  PUSHTYPE never sends your audio anywhere; the models run on your Mac.

We don't redistribute the weights, but we credit them anyway: it's what their authors ask for, and CC BY 4.0 requires it when a work is shared.

Typing models (downloaded on first use, not bundled)

TYPE, the inline autocomplete, runs one of these text models on your Mac through llama.cpp.  As with the speech models, PUSHTYPE downloads the file you pick from Hugging Face; we don't redistribute the weights.  Each is the quantized GGUF build by mradermacher of the original model, and every one is licensed under the Apache License 2.0 (checked on each model card, 2026-10-14).

When redistributing, pass on the agreement and include the notice "Licensed by NVIDIA Corporation under the NVIDIA Open Model License".  Rights end if you sue over the model's IP, or if you bypass its guardrails without a substitute.  Using NVIDIA's trademarks isn't granted: we name NVIDIA only to say where the model comes from, and never use its logo.

  • MIT (Silero VAD). Keep the copyright notice.
