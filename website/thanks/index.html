You're on the air.

Your PUSH licence key is in the email Polar just sent you. Here's how to start talking.

  1. Download PUSH if you haven't: get the latest version, drag it to Applications and open it.
  2. Copy your licence key from Polar's email.
  3. Click PUSH in the menu bar, open Settings ▸ Licence, paste the key and click Activate.

One key works on 3 Macs, and a fourth takes its own $10 key. Moving to a new Mac? Open Settings ▸ Licence on the old one and click Remove This Mac to free its place.
