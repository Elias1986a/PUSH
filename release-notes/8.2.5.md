## Changed

- **A simpler choice of speech models.** PUSH now offers three: Parakeet Streaming, Parakeet Ultra and Nemotron Multilingual. Parakeet Unified is gone. With PUSH transcribing while you talk, it was no faster and no more accurate than Ultra, and it only did English. If you used it, PUSH switches you to the recommended model, and its 600 MB download is removed to free the space.
- **The recommended model follows your Mac's language.** On an English Mac it's Parakeet Streaming, which shows your words in the pill as you speak. Anywhere else it's Parakeet Ultra, which understands English and the European languages and switches between them on its own. You can still pick any model in Settings ▸ Models.
