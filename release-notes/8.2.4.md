## Faster

- **Long dictation lands almost instantly.** PUSH now transcribes finished sentences while you're still talking, so letting go leaves only your last sentence or two. On a 25-second dictation, the wait after you let go drops from about a quarter of a second to a few hundredths.

## Fixed

- **"I mean" without a pause.** "15.2 seconds I mean 1.52 seconds" now pastes as "1.52 seconds" even when you don't pause before "I mean".
- **Times of day.** "4:30 pm" and "4:30 PM" paste as "4:30 p.m.", like the rest of PUSH.
