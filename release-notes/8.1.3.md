- **Spoken self-corrections are on by default:** "15.2 seconds, I mean 1.52 seconds" pastes as "1.52 seconds". Turn it off in Settings ▸ Text.
- **Missing question marks:** when the model leaves a question unpunctuated ("…Can you confirm before Friday"), PUSH adds the "?" — including right after a quotation.
- **Quotes after "said,":** `Priya said, quote, ship it anyway, end quote` → `Priya said, "Ship it anyway."`
- **Capital after a removed filler:** "…target. Um, latency…" → "…target. Latency…"
- **Numbers with units stay numbers:** "at 1.52 seconds" or "by 2.25 percent" no longer turn into clock times.
- **Spacing:** a correction in a later sentence no longer glues it to the sentence before.
