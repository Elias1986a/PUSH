## New

- **Drag to allow Accessibility.** When PUSH sends you to System Settings for Accessibility, a PUSH card now rides along at the bottom of the window. Drag PUSH from the card into the list and you're done: no hunting for the + button, no digging through Applications. The card follows the window, steps aside when you switch apps, and goes away by itself the moment access is on. The push-to-talk key starts working straight away, no restart needed.
