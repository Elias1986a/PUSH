## Better

- **Your words land more than twice as fast.** Text now appears about 0.13 seconds after you let go of the PUSH-to-talk key, down from about 0.35. The pill's glow was holding the app up for a moment after every dictation, and stopping the microphone now happens in the background.
- **Shorter setup.** Seven pages, eight with a license, down from ten. Choose Talk and TYPE on the welcome page; your name, About you, learning and screen choices are now on one page, "Make it yours".
- **Settings, rearranged.** TYPE's models now sit in Models beside the speech models, with one Storage section for both. The pill's position and live preview are in Talk.
- **Show my words as I speak** can be switched on in setup when you choose Parakeet Streaming.
- **TYPE keeps suggesting while you download a different model,** using the one you already have until the new one is ready.
- **Speech models download from a fixed, tested version,** so a change upstream can't reach you untested. Models you already have are kept.
- **The menu says what's missing.** "Needs Accessibility" or "Needs Microphone", with a Fix button, instead of "Ready" when the key can't work.
- **Diagnostic log** in Settings ▸ General, with Show in Finder and Clear, for when you write to support. It never records what you say or type, or which apps you use.
- Deleting a model asks first. Clearer wording, better VoiceOver labels, calmer animations with Reduce Motion, and tighter spacing throughout.

## Fixed

- **Two quick dictations no longer lose your clipboard.** The second one used to put the first one's text back on your clipboard instead of what you had copied.
- **TYPE works as soon as you grant Accessibility,** without restarting PUSHTYPE.
- **TYPE ignores every key while a password field has secure input on.**
- **Turning on the wake word no longer freezes the app** for a few seconds while the microphone starts.
- **A Mac with no microphone connected** no longer crashes when you press the key.
- **Microphone refused:** pressing the key now tells you how to fix it, instead of an alert at every launch.
- **No speech model yet:** the key waits for your download instead of starting one on its own.
- Setup's speech-model download has a Cancel button, and says so when nothing has arrived for 90 seconds.
