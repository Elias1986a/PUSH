## Fixed

- **Long dictation keeps its punctuation.** Past about 15 seconds, PUSH sometimes stopped adding full stops, commas and capitals partway through, so everything after that point also missed its cleanup. PUSH now splits long dictation where a sentence ends, which keeps the punctuation all the way through. On Parakeet Unified, the first word after each split is no longer dropped either.
- **"New paragraph" after a quote.** Saying "new line" or "new paragraph" right after "end quote" now breaks the line instead of typing the words.
- **Money in words.** "Five million dollars" pastes as `$5 million`, not `$5,000,000`.
- **Doubled full stops.** "seconds.." pastes as "seconds.". A real "..." is left alone.
- **California mode.** "Could you like send it" now drops the "like"; "would you like to come" and "I like cooking" keep theirs.
