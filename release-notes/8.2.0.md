## New

- **California mode** (Settings ▸ Text, off by default). Removes casual "like": "It was like really good" → "It was really good." Keeps "I like it", "looks like rain", "like I said", "like 30 minutes" and "he was like, no way."

## Changed

- **Apple Speech has been removed.** It was the least accurate engine in every benchmark. The models are now Parakeet Ultra (default), Parakeet Unified, Parakeet Streaming and Nemotron Multilingual. If Apple Speech was your choice, PUSH switches to Parakeet Ultra.

## Fixed

- **Emphasis isn't treated as a correction.** "It's cold, I mean very cold." used to paste as "Very cold."
- **"Scratch that"** no longer leaves a stray period at the start of the next sentence.
- **Corrected numbers are replaced whole.** "The budget is 5 million. Sorry, 6 million." → "The budget is 6 million."
- **A question after a quote gets its "?".** `Priya said, "Ship it anyway." Can you confirm before Friday?`
- **Quotes after "said," start with a capital.** `Priya said, "Ship it anyway."`
