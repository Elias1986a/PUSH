## Better

- **Spelling fixes catch more typos.** The fix stays on offer while you carry on typing the next word ("I will recieve i" → "I will receive i"), so you don't have to stop at the typo. Words you're still typing ("apprec…") are never treated as mistakes.
- **The right word.** Fixes now use the same choice as your Mac's own autocorrect, looking at the whole sentence, so "recive" becomes "receive", not "revive".
- **A space after the fix**, like an accepted suggestion, when you fix the word you just typed.
- **Spelling fixes in Claude Code and other agent prompts in Terminal.** Accept with your whole-suggestion key (` ~); Tab stays the terminal's.
