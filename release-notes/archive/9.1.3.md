## New

- **Type learns your habits.** When you finish a phrase the same way again and again ("Thanks for sending this over. I'll take a look tonight."), PUSHTYPE offers your wording as soon as you start it. Habits are learned from everything you write with "Learn from my writing and voice" on, and only after you've written something the same way at least three times, so a one-off detail from one conversation never turns up in another. Past writing on the same topic still comes only from the same window.

## Fixed

- **Quitting PUSHTYPE no longer ends in a crash.** With a typing model loaded, macOS could report that PUSHTYPE quit unexpectedly every time you quit.
