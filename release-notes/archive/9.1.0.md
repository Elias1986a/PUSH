## New

- **PUSH is now PUSHTYPE.** Same app, new name, for voice and typing. Your settings, permissions and downloaded models carry over.
- **Choose your accept keys.** In Settings ▸ Typing, pick the key that takes the next word (Tab by default) and the one that takes the whole suggestion (the ` ~ key by default). Keys you need for writing, like Return, Space and Esc, can't be used, and PUSHTYPE says why.
- **Suggestion color in the menu.** Switch colors straight from the menu bar when gray is hard to see on a see-through field or a busy wallpaper.
- **Learn from my writing and voice.** What you dictate where suggestions are on is learned too, as finished text and never audio. It's still off until you turn it on, encrypted, and only on this Mac.
- **Screen Recording gets its own welcome page**, explaining what it reads and that nothing is kept. If macOS asks to quit and reopen PUSHTYPE after you allow it, the welcome window comes back on the same page.
- **Downloaded typing models** show how much space they use, with Show in Finder.

## Changed

- **Type is on by default**, like Dictation. Until a typing model is downloaded, the menu says where to get one.
- **A shorter welcome:** two pages fewer. The pill's position is chosen on the Try it page, and the wake word and teleprompter are on the last page.
- **Settings:** the Text page's options (formatting, spoken corrections, California mode) are now in Dictation. The Dictionary says up front that it's used for both voice and typing.
- **The smallest typing model (Qwen3.5 0.8B) is gone.** If you used it, PUSHTYPE switches to Recommended.

## Fixed

- **The menu's arrow stays on the icon** when the icon changes, for example from the hourglass once the typing model is ready.
