## Better

- **Going back to fix a word no longer covers your text.** In Claude Code and other agent prompts in Terminal, when you move the cursor back into what you've written, the suggestion now appears in a small card below the line, as it already did in other apps, instead of on top of the words after the cursor.
- **No more repeats.** A suggestion never repeats the words that are already after your cursor. Going back after "I'm" in "I'm I don't know how…" offers "sure", not "sure I don't know how".
