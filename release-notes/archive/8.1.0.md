Everything since 8.0.2, now on the default **Parakeet Ultra**:

- **Spoken corrections are stricter and smarter.** "Tuesday, sorry, Wednesday" → Wednesday; "It costs 20, correction, 25 dollars" → 25 dollars; "before 7.0. 8.0, sorry." → before 8.0. Everyday phrases ("I mean," to start a sentence, "Sorry for the delay", "delete that file") are no longer touched, and long sentences after "I mean" no longer delete what came before.
- **Parakeet Ultra is the default** — English plus the Latin-script European languages, detected automatically. Nemotron Multilingual covers the rest.
- **Spoken quotes** — "quote … end quote", "quote unquote", and a "quote" that closes itself at the end of the sentence.
- **Clock times** written as 3:30.
- Parakeet TDT v2 removed; FluidAudio updated to 0.17.4.
