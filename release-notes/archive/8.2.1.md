## New

- **Choose your speech model during setup.** On first launch, the welcome wizard now has a "Choose a speech model" step. Parakeet Ultra is selected and recommended. Press Download, watch the progress, and continue once the model is ready. Setup can't be skipped until a model is on your Mac, since PUSH can't dictate without one.

## Changed

- **PUSH no longer downloads a speech model silently at first launch.** The 600 MB download now happens only when you choose it, in setup or in Settings ▸ Models. The small supporting models still load automatically.
