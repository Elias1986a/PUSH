## New

- **Type.** As you type in any app, PUSH suggests how your sentence goes on, in gray right at the cursor. Press Tab to take it a word at a time, or keep typing to ignore it. It works in Mail, Notes, Messages, TextEdit, web pages in Safari and Chrome, and Claude Code in Terminal. Like dictation, it runs entirely on this Mac: nothing you type leaves it.
- **Suggestions right after dictation.** Finish a dictated sentence and the next suggestion appears on its own, space included.
- **Pick your model.** Four sizes, from Qwen3.5 0.8B (630 MB) to Gemma 4 E4B (5.3 GB, for Macs with 16 GB or more). Bigger models write better; smaller ones are lighter on memory. Change it any time in Settings ▸ Typing.
- **Make it yours.** Choose the suggestion length and color, add a sign-off name and a note about how you write, and turn Type off in the apps where you don't want it. Words in your PUSH Dictionary finish themselves and are never treated as typos.
- **Learn from my writing** (Settings ▸ Personal, off by default). PUSH keeps what you write, encrypted and only on this Mac, and uses it so suggestions sound like you. Never synced; Delete All erases it.
- **Context helps.** What you just copied and the text around the field shape the suggestion. Neither is ever stored. Both can be turned off in Settings ▸ Typing.
- **Choose your features.** Dictation and Type can each be switched on or off, in the welcome window, in Settings, and in the menu.
