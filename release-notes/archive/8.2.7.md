## New

- **Pick your start sound** (Settings ▸ Dictation). Six sounds made for PUSH: Deep Tap (the new default), Mic Tap, Bright Tap, Key Up, and two 8-bit ones, Power On and Ready. Choosing one plays it. They replace the old radio chirp.

## Fixed

- **California mode and numbers.** "Like 30 minutes" still means *about* 30 and keeps its "like", but "like 90s themes" and "like 8-bit" now lose it: a decade or a style isn't a quantity.
