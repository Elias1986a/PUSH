## New

- **Better punctuation when you speak English.** Parakeet Streaming and Parakeet Ultra now come with a small punctuation model that puts periods, commas, question marks, and capitals where they belong.  It adds about 60 MB to the download and about 0.01 seconds after you let go.
- **The Oxford comma.** "Milk, eggs and bread" is now written "milk, eggs, and bread."
- **"Know," not "no."** When you say "I don't know" or "let me know," PUSHTYPE writes "know" wherever "no" can't be the word you meant.
- **More PUSH-to-talk keys.** Settings ▸ Talk ▸ Key now offers Right or Left Shift and Fn (Globe) as well as Option, Command, and Control.  Shift and Fn wait a quarter of a second before listening, so typing capitals never starts Talk.
- **Shortcuts don't trigger Talk.** Press another key while holding the PUSH-to-talk key (⌥Tab, ⌘C) and the recording is quietly dropped.
- **Pick TYPE's keys from a menu.** Settings ▸ TYPE has two menus, Next word and Whole suggestion, each offering Tab, ~, Right Arrow, and End.
- **Turn TYPE off in one app from the menu bar.** Under Suggestions there is now a checkbox for the app you were last typing in.  Uncheck it and TYPE stays off in that app until you check it again.  Pause still turns TYPE off everywhere for a while.
- **Rewriting (experimental).** An optional model in Settings ▸ Talk that fixes misheard words and grammar after you speak, in English.  It's a 1.1 GB download and adds up to a second and a half.

## Better

- **Tidier Settings.** The panes are in a new order (Personal, General, Talk, TYPE, Teleprompter, Dictionary, Models) with fewer, clearer sections.
- **Live text in the pill offers to switch for you.** Turning it on with a model other than Parakeet Streaming asks to switch to Streaming, downloading it if needed.

## Fixed

- Dictations that started with "Thanks for" or "Thank you" were sometimes thrown away.
