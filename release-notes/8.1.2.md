- **Decimals in self-corrections:** "latency came back at 15.2 seconds, I mean 1.52 seconds" now resolves to "…1.52 seconds" instead of garbling into "15. 1. 52 seconds". (Only applies with spoken self-corrections switched on.)
- **Fillers mid-text:** an "Um," that opens a later sentence is now removed, not just one at the very start.
- **"Umbrella" is safe:** text starting with words like "Umbrella" no longer loses its first letters to the filler remover.
